Delete VideoMate Account
Xóa tài khoản VideoMate

This page applies to the VideoMate Android app, package xyz.goirong.videomate, published by SGA VN.

Supported deletion method / Cách xóa được hỗ trợ

In VideoMate, open the Profile area, choose Pháp lý & an toàn (Legal & Safety), then choose Xóa tài khoản (Delete Account). Read the confirmation and select Xóa tài khoản again to complete the request. End any active call first: the server rejects deletion while the account is in a waiting, ringing, or active call.

This static support page does not collect deletion requests. Do not put account passwords or sensitive data into a GitHub issue. Use the in-app deletion flow to authenticate and delete the account.

What happens when an account is deleted / Dữ liệu được xử lý khi xóa

The implementation removes account credentials and refresh sessions; anonymizes or clears personal profile fields; removes the avatar and private chat image attachments when safe; replaces the User’s chat text with a neutral deletion marker; deletes nonessential blocks, likes, visits, tickets, and related interaction data; and marks the account as deleted so it cannot be used again.

Some minimal records may be retained only where necessary for Gold/gift/payment accounting and reconciliation, completed-call records, security, fraud prevention, moderation, dispute resolution, or legal obligations. Retained records are associated with an anonymized/deleted profile and do not reactivate the account. The audited source does not establish general fixed retention periods.

For more detail, read the VideoMate Privacy Policy.

Need help? / Cần hỗ trợ?

For deletion information, privacy, or support questions, submit a Privacy / Support inquiry. Do not include passwords, payment information, or identity documents.
