Delete VideoMate Account
Xóa tài khoản VideoMate

This page applies to the VideoMate Android app, package xyz.goirong.videomate, published by SGA VN.

Method A — In the app / Trong ứng dụng

In VideoMate, open Profile → Pháp lý & an toàn (Legal & Safety) → Xóa tài khoản (Delete Account) → confirm. End any active call first: the server rejects deletion while the account is in a waiting, ringing, or active call.

Method B — Without the app / Không cần ứng dụng

You can request account deletion without opening or reinstalling VideoMate. Select the button below to email VideoMate Support.

Yêu cầu xóa tài khoản
Request account deletion

Suggested email subject: VideoMate Account Deletion Request.

For your security, do not include your account password, Gmail password, OTP, payment-card information, access token, refresh token, or identity documents. VideoMate may request reasonable verification to help prevent unauthorized deletion. Identity documents are not requested unless legally necessary and separately justified.

What happens when an account is deleted / Dữ liệu được xử lý khi xóa

Deletion may revoke sessions, remove account credentials, clear or anonymize profile information, remove supported private media and attachments when safe, remove or anonymize nonessential interaction data, and mark the account as deleted so it cannot be used again. The implementation also replaces the User’s chat text with a neutral deletion marker.

Limited data may remain only when necessary for security, fraud prevention, moderation/safety, completed Gold/gift/payment accounting and reconciliation, completed-call records, dispute resolution, or legal/regulatory obligations. Retained records are associated with an anonymized/deleted profile and do not reactivate the account. The audited source does not establish a general fixed retention period.

For more detail, read the VideoMate Privacy Policy.

Need help? / Cần hỗ trợ?

For account deletion, privacy, or sensitive account support, email videomate.support@gmail.com. GitHub Issues are only for non-sensitive general questions; do not post personal or sensitive information in a public issue.
