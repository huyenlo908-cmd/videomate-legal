Hỗ trợ VideoMate

For account deletion, privacy inquiries, and sensitive account support, email videomate.support@gmail.com.

Privacy inquiries / Câu hỏi quyền riêng tư

Ask about this policy, the categories of data described here, permissions, sharing, retention, or your available choices at videomate.support@gmail.com.

Safety and reporting / An toàn và báo cáo

Use the in-app report and block tools whenever available to report content or another account. For sensitive account support, email videomate.support@gmail.com.

Account deletion / Xóa tài khoản

You may delete an account in VideoMate: Profile → Pháp lý & an toàn (Legal & Safety) → Xóa tài khoản (Delete Account) → confirm. You may also request deletion without the app by emailing videomate.support@gmail.com. You do not need to reinstall VideoMate. Review the Delete Account information.

GitHub Issues / Câu hỏi chung

GitHub Issues may be used only for non-sensitive general questions: open a public issue. Do not put personal or sensitive information in a public GitHub Issue, including passwords, payment information, identity documents, chat content, or account details.
